The Story

It was 8:14 PM
when the wrong order got picked.

Somewhere on the Gulf coast, a maintenance planner named Ahmed sat in front of a spreadsheet with 200 open work orders. Compressors, pumps, sensors, generators — every row looked urgent, because on paper, they all were.

He picked the top one. It turned out to be a routine filter change. Three floors down, a pressure sensor with a high safety risk sat untouched for another six hours — because it was buried on page four, sorted by order number instead of by what actually mattered.

Nothing happened that night. But it easily could have. And across every plant powering Saudi Arabia's industrial ambitions, this exact moment repeats itself, shift after shift.

That's the gap One Vision decided to close — not with another dashboard full of numbers, but with an assistant that thinks like a safety-first engineer, scores every order in real time, and simply tells the supervisor: start here.

The Problem

Prioritization is still a manual, risky guess.

⚠️

Safety hides in the noise

High-safety-risk orders don't stand out in a flat list — they're one row among hundreds, easy to miss under time pressure.

⏱️

Every minute has a cost

Downtime hours, cost-per-hour, and production impact all matter — but no one has time to mentally weigh three factors per order, 200 times a shift.

📋

Spreadsheets don't reason

Sorting by date or order number tells you nothing about urgency. Supervisors need judgment, not just a list.

🗣️

No natural way to ask

"What should my team tackle first, and why?" is a simple question — but no system could answer it in plain language, until now.

The Solution

One Source of truth. One ranked list.
Zero guesswork.

Our solution brings maintenance priorities into one clear view. Every order is evaluated based on safety risk, equipment criticality, and production impact, then automatically ranked so supervisors know what needs attention first. Joule makes decisions even easier by explaining priorities and answering questions in natural language, while SAP SAC provides a high-level view for decision-makers.

Architecture

From raw orders to a live, conversational ranking.

Maintenance orders flow into the SAP CAP service, which exposes a clean OData API. A Joule agent reads and reasons over that data, while a BDC/SAC-style dashboard visualizes the live, ranked queue for the supervisor.

Tech Stack

Built entirely on SAP's Business Technology Platform.

SAP
One integrated platform, three SAP services

SAP BTP

SAP Business Technology Platform is the foundation: hosting, security, and the connective layer that lets CAP, Joule and SAC work together as one solution.

Platform Foundation

SAP CAP

The Cloud Application Programming Model powers the core service: a CDS data model, an OData API, and server-side business logic that computes priority on every create/update.

Core Service Layer

SAP Joule

SAP's AI copilot connects to the CAP service, letting supervisors ask anything in natural language and get a grounded, explained answer.

Conversational AI Layer

SAP SAC

SAP Analytics Cloud turns the ranked, live order queue into visual, at-a-glance dashboards for supervisors and plant managers — no spreadsheets required.
